# Release member kinds

`beam.ux.release-member-kinds` holds the **kinds** a Release member can be. Each entry is a
`ReleaseMemberKind`, keyed by the `member_kind` its members store. beam-ux ships `entry`, a
page-editor entry; a package whose subject a Release can publish registers its own kind.

## What a kind owns

The Release owns what every member shares: its row lock, its lifecycle, review, scheduling and
Preview Links. A kind owns its subject:

- how a member's `member_ref` resolves, and the refusal of a subject outside the Release's realm or
  connection;
- who may place, approve, publish and record it (`ReleaseAccess`: edit, publish, direct);
- whether a member may join, and whether its subject moved after it joined;
- what Publish does to its members, after every member of every kind has passed;
- what a rollback Release selects for it.

A member's `version_id` is the version the Release publishes, or null to withdraw the subject.

## What ships

- `entry` is a page-editor entry, addressed by its id (also stored as `entry_id`). A null version is
  a Take-down, and only a Take-down carries a redirect target.

## Adding a kind

```php
use Splicewire\Beam\Ux\Release\Kinds\ReleaseMemberKindRegistry;

app(ReleaseMemberKindRegistry::class)->register(new ConfigOverrideMemberKind);
```

A taken key throws: stored members name their kind, so replacing one would change what existing
rows mean. A Release holding a member of a kind no installed package provides refuses to advance.
